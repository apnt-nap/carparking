The problem

On weekends, drivers at big Bangkok malls circle floor after floor looking for a space. The mall knows how many bays are free, but the driver doesn't, until they're already inside.

How it works

Three tabs: Map, Parking & Pay, and Account, in Thai and English, light and dark mode.

Find a mall with space

The map shows each mall with its live free count, coloured green, amber or red. Search by text or voice: "พารากอน", "icon siam".

Pick the best floor

Floors are sorted by free spaces, with EV, accessible and family bays counted. The best floor is tagged.

Drive to a free bay

A live floor plan suggests the nearest free bay to the ramp or to the mall entrance, and draws the route along one-way lanes.

Leave without a ticket

The gate reads your registered plate. The fee builds up in the app and is paid from your balance. PDPA consent first.

Malls in the demo

Five sample malls with 21 parking floors and about 3,200 simulated bays. Occupancy changes every few seconds, like real sensors.

Sensors only

We install bay sensors and gateways. Best for malls with no guidance system yet.

API ready

The mall's parking system already counts spaces. We connect to its API, so setup is much cheaper.

Ticketless entry

Optional link to the gate's plate reader, so registered cars enter and leave with no paper ticket.

Demo script (2 minutes)

A click path for the presentation. Open the app demo on a phone or in the frame above.

  1. Map: point out the coloured labels and live free counts. Tap the search bar to see the 3 nearest malls, then type พารากอน or tap the mic.
  2. Mall: tap Siam Paragon, then Choose a spot. Floors are sorted by free spaces, with the best one tagged.
  3. Floor plan: open the best floor. Show the suggested bay and route, switch to Near the mall entrance, then tap another green bay. Watch bays change live. Tap I parked here.
  4. Account: top up ฿300 with the PromptPay placeholder, then Set up ticketless entry: tick both boxes and add plate 1กก 1234.
  5. Parking & Pay: in Account, Add demo e-ticket (entered 200 minutes ago). Show the running fee, tick I bought something to apply free hours, then pay and simulate the exit gate.
  6. Settings: switch to ไทย and to dark mode to finish.

Tip: Account → Reset demo data before you present. Data stays in the browser only.
